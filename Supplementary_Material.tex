\section{Theoretical Details}
\subsection{Privacy of the Complete Adaptive Release}
For the complete release map in main III-A, let $K(z,A)\in[0,1]$ be its conditional release probability given the accounted teacher output $z$. Integrating the DP inequality gives $\int K\,dP_D\le e^\varepsilon\int K\,dP_{D'}+\delta$~\cite{dwork2014}. This includes adaptive checkpoint/route selection and student training, conditional on permitted side information. Adaptively choosing Hard retains selection's private dependence; all inspected private training must be accounted.


\subsection{Balanced Softmax: A Pre-clipping Identity}
Differentiation gives $(p_y-1)h$ for CE and $(q_y-1)h$ for BS~\cite{ren2020balanced}, where $q_y=\nu_yp_y/\sum_j\nu_jp_j$. Thus $\nu_y<\sum_j\nu_jp_j$ increases the target-row norm for $h\ne0$, before clipping; both norms vanish at $h=0$. Training-time logit adjustment with $\tau=1$ equals BS~\cite{menon2021longtail}.

For CV17's linear head including bias, the complete gradient is the outer product $(q-e_y)[h;1]^\top$. Any probability vector satisfies $\|q-e_y\|_2\le\sqrt2$. Unit-normalized features therefore give Frobenius norm at most $\sqrt2\sqrt{1+\|h\|_2^2}=2$, explaining the chosen clipping norm. The same bound holds for CE with $p$ in place of $q$. It is specific to the normalized linear head, not the earlier mel-CNNs; those models' recorded clipping fractions can be near one without contradicting this bound.

\subsection{Health Estimation and Region Probabilities}
For a fixed teacher and independent calibration draws, let $\theta_{ij}=\Pr(Y=i,\widehat Y_t=j)$ and $\widehat\theta=C/n$. Write $r_k=\sum_j\theta_{kj}$, $c_k=\sum_i\theta_{ik}$, and $d_k=\theta_{kk}$. Assume $r_k>0$ and a unique maximum column $k^*$ when $\lambda_3>0$. The delta method gives
\begin{equation}
\widehat\sigma_H^2=\frac1n\nabla H(\widehat\theta)^\top
\big[\operatorname{Diag}(\widehat\theta)-\widehat\theta\widehat\theta^\top\big]
\nabla H(\widehat\theta).
\label{eq:health_se}
\end{equation}
The derivatives are
\begin{align}
\frac{\partial B}{\partial\theta_{ab}}
&=\frac{\mathbf1\{a=b\}r_a-d_a}{K r_a^2},\\
\frac{\partial F}{\partial\theta_{ab}}
&=\frac1K\sum_k\left[\frac{2\mathbf1\{a=b=k\}}{r_k+c_k}\right.\nonumber\\
&\hspace{5mm}\left.-\frac{2d_k(\mathbf1\{a=k\}+\mathbf1\{b=k\})}{(r_k+c_k)^2}\right],\\
\frac{\partial(1-M)}{\partial\theta_{ab}}&=-\mathbf1\{b=k^*\}.
\end{align}
Combine them as $\nabla H=\lambda_1\nabla B+\lambda_2\nabla F+\lambda_3\nabla(1-M)$. Macro-F1's expression $2d_k/(r_k+c_k)$ avoids a false singularity at zero true positives. Tied maxima and same-development selection fall outside this regular fixed-teacher approximation.

\begin{proposition}[Confidence margins and routing stability]
\label{prop:routing_stability}
For the Gaussian working model, $\widehat\sigma_H>0$, and normal quantile $z_p$, the exact KD and Hard region certificates are
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
\widehat H-z_{1-\rho}\widehat\sigma_H&\ge\tau_{\mathrm{high}} &&(\mathrm{KD}),\\
\widehat H+z_{1-\rho}\widehat\sigma_H&\le\tau_{\mathrm{low}} &&(\mathrm{Hard}).
\end{aligned}
\label{eq:confidence_margins}
\end{equation}
HAKD requires its full interval mass to be at least $1-\rho$. Off both thresholds, let $d=\min\{|\widehat H-\tau_{\mathrm{low}}|,|\widehat H-\tau_{\mathrm{high}}|\}$. Then
\begin{equation}
1-q_{m_0(\widehat H)}\le\min\!\left\{1,2\exp\!\left(-\frac{d^2}{2\widehat\sigma_H^2}\right)\right\},
\label{eq:stability}
\end{equation}
so $q_{m_0(\widehat H)}\to1$ as $\widehat\sigma_H\to0$.
\end{proposition}
Inverting the Gaussian CDF gives the margins. Leaving the point estimate's region requires a deviation of at least $d$; the two-sided normal tail bound proves stability. The masses partition the real line without truncation. At zero SE, deterministic threshold ties are a software convention, not a unit-mass Gaussian limit. The result bounds region disagreement, not student gain.

The intermediate certificate depends on both tails. For fixed SE, the Gaussian interval mass is maximized at the region midpoint. Thus no HAKD certificate exists when $\widehat\sigma_H>(\tau_{\mathrm{high}}-\tau_{\mathrm{low}})/(2z_{1-\rho/2})$. At the default width 0.15 and $\rho=0.10$, this SE limit is approximately 0.0456. Smaller SE permits certification, but the observed health must still be sufficiently far from each boundary. These are properties of the working Gaussian, not calibration guarantees for future speakers.

Accounting assumes add/remove-one-record adjacency and permitted fixed class-count metadata; student post-processing adds no budget.

\paragraph*{Published CTKD mechanism}
Global-T CTKD~\cite{li2023ctkd} learns $T=1+20\operatorname{sigmoid}(u)$, initialized at $u=1$. At epoch $e$, $u$'s backward multiplier is $-\gamma_e$, with $\gamma_e=[1-\cos(\pi\min(e,10)/10)]/2$; gradients include $T^2$. CE/KL weights are $0.3/0.7$. Student and temperature share AdamW $(10^{-3},10^{-4})$ for learning rate/weight decay, replacing the image optimizer while preserving its mechanism and ten-epoch ramp. VCTK uses five epochs; the small Common Voice archive uses 15, CV17 80 (with its main V optimizer). Temperature, loss, and gradients match the official implementation in float64.\footnote{\url{https://github.com/zhengli97/CTKD}, commit \texttt{5611289}.}
